Project Overview

The Personal Finance Tracker is a Node.js command-line application that helps users take control of their money. Users can record income and expenses, sort them into categories and view clear summaries of their spending, with all data saved in MongoDB.

The Problem

Many people struggle to keep track of where their money goes. Spreadsheets feel tedious and many finance apps are cluttered. There was a need for a quick, simple way to log and review spending right from the terminal.

Key Features

  • Add and delete income and expenses
  • Organise spending by category
  • View balance and spending summaries
  • Colourful, easy-to-read terminal output

Technologies Used

What I Learned

I learned how to connect a Node.js app to MongoDB and perform CRUD operations, handle user input in the terminal, and use Chalk to make output clear and readable. It also improved how I structure and organise my code.
